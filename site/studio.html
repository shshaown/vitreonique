THE APPROACH

Start with the thing
worth paying attention to.

Every project needs a clear center: a product, an idea, or a story. The website should help people understand it, then give them a reason to stay curious.

This portfolio brings that approach to life through Vitreonique GX–8, a study in making complex forms approachable through interaction.

HOW A PROJECT TAKES SHAPE

Clear thinking.
Expressive execution.

01 / DISCOVER

Understand the story.

Define the audience, the message, and the moment that matters most.

02 / DESIGN

Set the direction.

Build a visual language and page structure that makes the subject feel distinctive.

03 / DEVELOP

Make it tangible.

Translate the design into a responsive, working experience with purposeful interaction.

04 / REFINE

Consider the details.

Review the layout and core interactions, then prepare the site for presentation.
